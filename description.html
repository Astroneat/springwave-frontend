Clean Mỹ Khê’s Beach is a meaningful volunteer activity that helps protect the environment and raise awareness about marine pollution.

Clean Mỹ Khê’s Beach is a very useful activity for everyone to join.

Volunteers will collect trash along the coastline, separate recyclable waste, and help educate tourists about keeping beaches clean.

The event also provides an opportunity for students and local citizens to connect, collaborate, and contribute positively to the community.

By participating in this activity, you will gain volunteer experience, teamwork skills, and contribute to making Da Nang a cleaner city.
