SpringWave utilizes our custom RAG/LLM engine to evaluate your survey responses across four criteria: Communication, Technical, Creativity, and Social Impact. It compares your profile against our extracurricular catalog to recommend activities that align with your career goals and skill gap recommendations.

Every time you engage with the community — starting a thread in the forum, replying to a discussion, or participating in university clubs — you earn Experience Points (XP). Unlocking certain XP thresholds increases your level and unlocks 16 uniquely-colored badges showcased on your profile.
