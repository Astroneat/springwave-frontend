Built by students, for students — a platform that helps you discover opportunities, connect with communities, and grow beyond the classroom.

psychology Why SpringWave?

Students often miss out on opportunities because information about workshops, competitions, extracurricular activities, and academic programs is scattered across dozens of fanpages, Facebook groups, and websites. We built SpringWave to change that.
