About Us

We are
SpringWave.

Built by students, for students — a platform that helps you discover opportunities, connect with communities, and grow beyond the classroom.

The Narrative
01 / The Problem

Scattered information leaves students in the dark.

Students often miss out on workshops, extracurricular activities, and academic opportunities simply because information is fragmented across dozens of Facebook pages, emails, and group chats.

02 / The Solution

A single hub to discover your trajectory.

SpringWave aggregates events, workshops, and student forums into a single, clean platform. Combined with personality quizzes and personalized recommendations, it helps students discover their next step with clarity.
